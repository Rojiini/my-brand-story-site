# Contact Us and newsletters

## Upload and activate

Upload the complete ZIP through Shopify Admin → Online Store → Themes → Add theme → Upload ZIP. Preview before publishing.

Shopify themes cannot create store pages or change your store’s email settings. In Online Store → Pages, create a page titled **Contact Us**, with handle `contact`, and select the **contact** theme template in the uploaded theme. The header and footer automatically show its link when the page exists (the main menu remains editable).

## Messages

The native Shopify contact form collects name, email, topic, and message. Shopify validates/processes submissions and handles its spam protection. Contact inquiries are sent to the store’s configured sender/contact email; confirm this address in Shopify Settings → Notifications. The displayed gallery address is info@hichgallery.com, but theme code does not change the store’s notification recipient. A message does not subscribe its sender to marketing.

## Newsletter audience

The separate native Shopify customer form collects email and optional first/last name fields, tagged `newsletter` (or `event-notifications` on Events). An unchecked, required marketing-consent checkbox explains newsletters and unsubscribe. Shopify manages customer email subscription status and any configured double opt-in. Optional name-field persistence must be confirmed on a submitted customer profile in your store; do not assume newsletter form fields save every profile detail.

Subscribers are in **Customers → Email subscribers**. Use **Shopify Messaging** (Shopify’s email marketing app) to compose/schedule newsletters for that segment; install/enable it if needed. Provider pricing, sender setup, and sending allowances are managed in Shopify, not by this theme. Do not send newsletters to customers who have not opted in. Shopify handles unsubscribe for its campaigns.

## Preview and verification

The Lovable visual reference hands forms to Shopify's contact endpoint and never displays a pretend success. Shopify can redirect to a CAPTCHA challenge; the uploaded native theme includes Shopify’s full form and anti-spam integration. Cross-origin preview submissions are not a substitute for testing the installed theme.

After installing, send an inquiry from an address you own, confirm receipt in the configured mailbox, submit a newsletter signup, complete confirmation if prompted, and confirm the customer is in Email subscribers. These hosted outcomes cannot be verified from an uninstalled ZIP. No newsletter campaign is sent by publishing this theme.
